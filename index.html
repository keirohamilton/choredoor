About Us

We know
the rules
because we
wrote them.

My name is Keith Hamilton. I retired as a Captain after 33 years of service with the Florida Division of Alcoholic Beverages and Tobacco (ABT). As a public servant, I worked throughout the state navigating some of the most complex and turbulent situations the Division has ever faced, from governmental pressures on local businesses to leveling the playing field for alcohol and tobacco operators across Florida.

For many years I spoke with the authority of the Division. Today I speak with the knowledge of it, and a better understanding of what the industry truly wants, so I can deliver exactly what it needs.

That spirit of public service is what drives us. We believe local businesses are the backbone of every community, and it is our mission to help them grow, stay compliant, and thrive. Our team is an extension of your business, we guide you as you grow, consult on the challenges you face, and find solutions that fit your specific needs.

Together, Keith Hamilton and Christine Gazard bring a combined 65 years of experience across Alcoholic Beverages and Tobacco, Hotels, and Restaurants. Almost any situation imaginable within the Division, we have been through it.

When your staff serves an underage or overly intoxicated patron, it is your business that suffers. Our state certification focuses on the real-world "what-if" scenarios of your industry, strengthening your staff's ability to make the right decisions and educating them on Florida's rules and regulations.

Training as a Responsible Vendor ensures your entire staff is better equipped, enhancing your business experience inside and out. It elevates your standing as a business that operates to a higher standard, positively affecting your legal standing in the courts and the cost of your insurance.
